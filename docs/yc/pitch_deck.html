Slide 02 • The Problem

AI Training Pipelines Face Two Broken Choices

When legacy NSFW classifiers trigger on a visual training sample, today's pipelines either delete human culture or poison model weights:

❌ Failure Mode 1: Sample-Dropping Bias

  • Coarse classifiers (NudeNet, OpenNSFW, LAION-Safety) make whole-image binary rejections.
  • 15%–40% false-positive rate on classical sculpture, figurative painting, anatomical studies, and garment folds (Riccio et al., arXiv:2409.17156).
  • Result: Millions of legitimate artistic & anatomical samples are erased from frontier training sets.

❌ Failure Mode 2: Inpainting Poisoning

  • Pipelines that attempt to "heal" sensitive regions via diffusion inpainting pass the image through a lossy VAE.
  • Mutates surrounding pixels (exterior_diff > 0), shifts colors, and hallucinates synthetic plastic textures.
  • Result: Training future models on diffusion-inpainted data triggers Model Autophagy Disorder (MAD).
Slide 03 • The Solution

Preservation-Aware Contrastive Curation

Instead of binary deletion or generative distortion, heav3ns-censor evaluates context, preserves valid visual signal bit-identically, and exports contrastive safety ground truth:

1. Artistic Nudity Pass

Classical silhouettes, warm skin gradients, and benign garment creases are preserved 100% bit-identically and annotated with Verified Benign Hard-Negative Boxes (class_id = 0).

2. Surgical SDF Redaction

When 1.5% of an image requires intervention, analytical rotated SDF masks (Rect, Ellipse, Pill) redact only that region while keeping 98.5% of the canvas 100% untouched (0 px diff).

3. Contrastive SFT & DPO

Exports paired positive (class_id = 1) and hard-negative (class_id = 0) boxes into MS-COCO, HuggingFace JSONL, VLM SFT, and Contrastive DPO preference triples.

Slide 05 • Curing Classifier False Positives

Contrastive Dual-Polarity Grounding & DPO Export

The industry is plagued by false positives because datasets only label what to censor—never what high-frequency confusers to leave alone.

Automated Confuser Hardness (fp_hardness_score)

  • Scores unredacted regions (5.0–99.5) using spatial gradient energy + warm skin-tone chrominance ratio.
  • Classifies confusers into composite_skin_and_crease_confuser, skin_tone_anatomical_silhouette, and high_frequency_linework_or_seam.
  • Hardness-Weighted NMS (IoU < 0.45) & Polarity De-Confliction (IoU < 0.20) guarantee clean training splits.
