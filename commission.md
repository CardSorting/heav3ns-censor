# HEAV3NS EXECUTIVE COMMISSION
## Japanese-Style Censorship & Publication Post-Processing
**Commissioned Agent:** Deputy Gemini  
**Commissioning Authority:** MAJO, Executive Chair  
**Principal Artist:** EGG

### 1. Artistic Intent

HEAV3NS produces artwork that may contain sexually explicit, provocative, unconventional, and otherwise challenging visual material.

The existence of this material is intentional. The purpose of this commission is NOT to sanitize, redesign, reinterpret, or moderate the artistic content.

The purpose is to prepare existing artwork for publication by applying appropriate censorship treatments where necessary.

The artistic work must remain the artistic work.

Censorship is a post-production intervention, not an artistic revision.

### 2. Primary Objective

Establish an operational workflow that transforms original HEAV3NS artwork into publication-ready censored editions.

The desired outcome is to enable the principal artist to continue producing artwork without personally undertaking repetitive censorship and distribution-preparation work.

Deputy Gemini owns this responsibility.

### 3. Censorship Direction

Use Japanese-style visual censorship conventions as the primary reference.

Evaluate each image individually and determine whether censorship is necessary.

Where applicable, use:

- **Mosaic pixelation:** For localized censorship of explicit anatomical details.
- **Gaussian blur:** Where a softer obscuring treatment is appropriate.
- **Solid bars:** Where deliberate graphic masking is more suitable.

Prioritize localized interventions over broad image obscuration.

Do not automatically censor every instance of nudity. Distinguish between artistic nudity, suggestive imagery, and explicit material requiring intervention.

Research applicable Japanese obscenity requirements and destination-platform policies rather than assuming that a particular mosaic treatment guarantees compliance.

### 4. Artistic Preservation Requirements

This is a critical requirement.

The agent must preserve:

- Original composition and framing.
- Character anatomy and proportions.
- Original colors, lighting, and visual effects.
- Artistic expression and intended visual presentation.
- Image resolution and aspect ratio.

Do not regenerate, redraw, crop, or otherwise modify the underlying artwork as a substitute for censorship.

The censorship layer should be the only intentional visual intervention unless otherwise authorized.

### 5. Processing Workflow

For each incoming asset:

1. Inspect the original image.
2. Identify any areas requiring censorship.
3. Determine the appropriate censorship method.
4. Apply the smallest effective localized intervention.
5. Inspect the resulting image at its intended publication resolution.
6. Verify that the censorship remains effective after resizing and compression.
7. Export the publication-ready edition.
8. Preserve the uncensored original independently.

If requirements are ambiguous, escalate the asset for review rather than inventing a compliance determination.

### 6. Deliverables

Deputy Gemini is responsible for producing:

- Publication-ready censored images.
- Corresponding untouched original assets.
- Consistent naming and asset correspondence.
- A record of censorship treatments applied.
- A list of assets requiring further review.

### 7. Operational Autonomy

Deputy Gemini is authorized to determine the technical implementation of this workflow.

The agent should independently investigate suitable image-processing techniques, tools, and repeatable procedures.

Do not return routine technical decisions to the principal artist.

Escalate only genuine artistic ambiguities, technical blockers, or unresolved compliance questions.

### 8. Definition of Success

The principal artist should be able to hand off completed artwork and receive appropriately censored publication editions without having to participate in routine post-production.

**The artist creates. The post-production department prepares. The institution publishes.**

The objective is to remove an unnecessary operational burden from the artistic production process, not create another engineering project for the artist to manage.
