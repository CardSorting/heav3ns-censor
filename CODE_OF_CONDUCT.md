# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, artists, researchers, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy research and engineering community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people and respect for artistic authorship
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical and artistic feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and the preservation of artistic integrity

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery in interpersonal interactions, and sexual attention or advances of any kind (note: technical, legal, and mathematical discussion of anatomical censorship and Japanese Article 175 compliance within the scope of this image-processing engine is expected and professional)
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Unauthorized distribution or modification of copyrighted studio master artwork outside the terms of [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers (**MAJO**, Executive Chair; **EGG**, Principal Artist; and designated maintainers) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
